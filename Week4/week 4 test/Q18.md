# Prompt A — Consistent Professional Response

Write a professional response to a customer whose order is delayed.

Requirements:

* Use a polite and professional tone.
* Keep the response concise.
* Acknowledge the delay.
* Show understanding of the customer's concern.
* Avoid creative language or unnecessary details.
* Produce a clear and consistent customer-support response.

# Output
```
We apologize for the delay with your order and understand how frustrating this may be. We appreciate your patience and are working to have your order delivered as soon as possible. Thank you for your understanding.

```
# Prompt B — Creative Response

Write a response to a customer whose order is delayed.

Requirements:

* Remain polite and professional.
* Acknowledge the customer's frustration.
* Use a more creative and engaging writing style.
* Avoid sounding repetitive or generic.
* You may use varied wording and a warmer tone.
* Keep the response concise.

#Output
```
We’re sorry your order has taken longer than expected. We understand how frustrating it is to wait for something you were expecting, and we truly appreciate your patience. We’re keeping an eye on the delay and appreciate your understanding.

```
# Prompting Requirements Influence the Output

The two prompts have the same basic task, but they emphasize different output goals.

| Aspect          | Prompt A     | Prompt B              |
| --------------- | ------------ | --------------------- |
| Tone            | Professional | Professional + warmer |
| Creativity      | Low          | Higher                |
| Consistency     | High         | Lower                 |
| Variation       | Lower        | Higher                |
| Professionalism | High         | High                  |
# Evaluation


| Criterion       | What to Compare                                       |
