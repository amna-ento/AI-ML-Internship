# Prompt
**Task**

Summarize the following text:

"Artificial intelligence is being used in healthcare, finance, education, and transportation. Companies use AI to automate repetitive tasks and analyze large amounts of data. However, organizations must consider privacy, security, bias, and reliability."

Follow all of these requirements:

* Use fewer than 80 words.
* Use simple English.
* Return exactly 3 bullet points.
* Do not add any information that is not stated in the original text.
* Do not include personal opinions.

Return only the 3 bullet-point summary.

# output
```
- AI is used in healthcare, finance, education, and transportation.
- Companies use AI to automate repetitive tasks and analyze large amounts of data.
- Organizations must consider privacy, security, bias, and reliability.

```
