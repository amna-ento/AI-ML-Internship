# Prompt 1

Summarize this text:

"Artificial intelligence is transforming many industries. In healthcare, it can help doctors analyze medical data and support diagnosis. In finance, AI can detect fraud and analyze financial patterns. In education, AI can provide personalized learning experiences. However, organizations must consider issues such as privacy, security, bias, and reliability when using AI."

# Output 1
```
AI is transforming industries like healthcare, finance, and education through diagnosis support, fraud detection, and personalized learning. However, its use requires attention to privacy, security, bias, and reliability.

```
