# Prompt
**Role**

You are a customer support analysis assistant.

**Task**

Analyze the following unstructured customer information and extract the required information.

Customer information:

"Ali ordered a laptop on August 12. Delivery was expected on August 15 but today is August 17. Tracking says delayed. He contacted support once and wants to know when it will arrive. He is not angry."

Use only the information provided. Do not invent or assume information that is not stated.

Determine whether escalation is required based on the information provided. Consider the delayed delivery, previous support contact, customer sentiment, and requested action.

# Output
```
Customer: Ali
Order Status: Delayed
Problem: Delivery is delayed
Sentiment: Not angry
Requested Action: Wants to know when it will arrive
Escalation Required: No

```
